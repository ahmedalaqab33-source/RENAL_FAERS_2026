# Research roadmap

These are candidate studies built on the private, verified FAERS pipeline. **No results are published.**
- Executed analyses so far are labelled EXPLORATORY methods pilots.
- Protocols are drafted *after* the exploratory pilot. They are **not** pre-registrations.

| Rank | Study | Question | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (flagship) | **Report- vs case-level selection** | How much do follow-up duplicates (13.85% of reports, 2013–2025) change renal disproportionality estimates, CIs and FDR classifications? | Pilot executed for 3 pre-specified pairs and independently verified. Expanding the hypothesis family needs PI approval |
| 2 | Drug identity and salt policy | How do unresolved primary-suspect exposures (about 14.9% of rows) and salt/base mapping change exposure and estimates? | Measurement complete (exposure counts, crosswalk verification). Policy and mapping review pending |
| 3 | Renal terminology eras | How do MedDRA version changes ("Acute kidney injury" absent before 2014) affect trends and disproportionality? | Era effect quantified. Needs licensed LLT→PT harmonisation and clinical endpoint approval |
| 4 | Robustness bundle | Sensitivity to FDA deleted cases, sparse-cell methods, bootstrap failures and multiplicity choices | Mostly sensitivity analyses of Study 1. Best reported inside Study 1, not as a separate paper |

Studies 1 and 4 overlap heavily, and Study 2 partly overlaps with Study 1's sensitivity analyses. Realistically this is **one strong methods paper (Study 1, including Study 4's analyses) plus one or two companion studies (2, 3)**, not four independent papers.

## Gates before any publication
- PI approval of the hypothesis family, endpoint, salt policy and deleted-case policy.
- Mapping accuracy review.
- Independent human methods review.
- A target-journal checklist (e.g. READUS-PV).
