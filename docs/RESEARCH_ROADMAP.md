# Research questions and approval gates

| Workstream | Question | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Report versus case selection | How does global follow-up selection affect exploratory reporting-association estimates? | Private methods pilot executed; interpretation and expanded hypothesis family on HOLD |
| Ingredient identity | How do missing identities and salt/base policies affect exposure definitions? | Private engineering checks exist; human mapping review and policy signoff pending |
| Terminology over time | How should licensed version-linked terminology harmonization address historical changes? | Endpoint and clinical review pending |
| Robustness | How sensitive are results to deleted-case policy, sparse cells and multiplicity choices? | Exploratory sensitivity work; formal policy approval pending |

Protocols drafted after exploration are not preregistrations. Workstreams overlap and do not imply separate publishable studies. No effect estimate, drug ranking or approved finding is released. PI approval, clinical/pharmacy review, independent human methods review and a target-journal reporting checklist remain required.
